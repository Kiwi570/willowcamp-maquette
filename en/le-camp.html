Do not feed

Why we don't feed them

The animals at Willow Camp follow a precise diet. A well-meant gesture can poison an animal within hours: a single carrot can kill a horse in 2 hours if it is excluded from its diet, and dry bread is very bad for all animals.

Some may look tired to you: they are old and receive suitable care. Please don't disturb their peace.

The wild part

Some areas are deliberately left untended to encourage biodiversity and pollination. They offer a protected refuge for wildlife and plants.

In May, the frogs sing all night long. Sensitive to noise? Bring earplugs: the geese can be chatty too.

Right next door: the Grand Brou nature reserve, a Natura 2000 site.

Living together at the camp

A few simple rules, so everyone enjoys the quiet, people and animals alike.

  1. Quiet comes first, especially in the evening: curfew at 10:30 pm. to be confirmed
  2. Children stay under an adult's supervision; under 12, never alone in the washrooms or at the playground.
  3. Drive at walking pace: 10 km/h. Once unloaded, the car goes to the car park.
  4. Dogs on a lead, never left alone, and not inside buildings.
  5. Barbecues and fires only in the shared area, no chemical firelighters.
  6. We sort: compost, PMC, paper and card, glass and household waste.
  7. We don't feed the animals, no exceptions.
  8. No swimming in the ponds.
  9. Groups only with prior agreement; the entry code is personal.

Frequently asked questions

What's the atmosphere like?

A natural, peaceful and friendly place. People come to reconnect, enjoy the quiet and share good moments, with respect for everyone.

Do I need to book ahead?

Yes, it's strongly recommended, especially in high season. Without booking it's sometimes possible depending on availability: contact the camp before you travel.

What time can I arrive?

Between 3:30 and 6:30 pm (7 pm on Fridays, Saturdays and days before public holidays), while reception is open. Not earlier or later: between noon departures and 3:30 pm, the camp is being prepared for you. Every family checks in at reception before settling in.

Are groups accepted?

Yes, only with the management's prior agreement. A group leader is appointed as the contact person during the stay. The entry code is personal.

Is the camp suitable for families?

Yes: playground, pétanque and animals, children love it. They stay under the supervision of the adults with them.

Can I swim or fish?

Swimming in the ponds is not allowed. Fishing is free for campers, and boating is only allowed with the camp's boat.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, on a lead: €5 per night on pitches, €10 in a hut. They are not allowed in the hiker cabins or in buildings.

Can I drive around the camp?

As little as possible: only vehicles you sleep in stay on the pitch. Speed limit 10 km/h.
