Our DNA

Here, we slow down.

Tucked away in the heart of Walloon Brabant, Willow Camp is a small haven of peace. A place to switch off from everyday life and reconnect with nature, with yourself and with others.

Read, draw, meditate, wander, enjoy a coffee or a tea, and let nature become your priority again.

Formerly Camping au Val-Tourinnes, then O Pas Sage and Présentement Camping: the place has been called Willow Camp since December 2022.

What there is, and what there isn't

There is

  • tall trees and willows
  • flower meadows, orchards, a vegetable garden
  • two ponds, free fishing with the camp's rowing boat
  • meadows, a wood, footpaths
  • lots of animals
  • a playground and a pétanque court
  • a shared eating area: barbecue, plancha, campfire
  • washrooms for your comfort

There isn't

  • flashy water slides
  • entertainment programmes
  • restaurant
  • advertising parasols
  • swimming in the ponds

Village supermarket 5 min away by car.

The neighbours have names

Donkeys, horses, billy goats, a pig, geese, hens and ducks live at the camp. Some are old and enjoy a peaceful retirement here.

Do not feed
